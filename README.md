Creative Fabrica is an Amsterdam-based digital marketplace and subscription service for crafters, designers and makers, offering more than 12 million commercial-use creative assets — fonts, graphics, illustrations, SVGs, embroidery designs, print-on-demand (POD) templates, 3D-printing files, laser-cutting files, paper-craft downloads and KDP interiors — plus an online design tool (Studio), AI generation tools, classes and bundles. The company operates the Creative Fabrica store, the Open Store marketplace for independent designers, the Studio web app and the The Artistry editorial blog. Chamber of Commerce 70114412 (NL), VAT NL858147877B01, contact hi@creativefabrica.com.

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Creative Fabrica: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
